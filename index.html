⚠️ This is a parent-facing play activity inspired by the structure of professional tools (MacArthur-Bates CDI word checklists; age-anchored concept items in the spirit of ASQ-3 / PLS-5 style screeners). If you ever have concerns about language development, talk to your pediatrician or ask about a free early-intervention evaluation.
